# Bring STMPCK into Apparat while preserving standalone play

Key: `[ ]` pending task, `[x]` completed task, `[?]` needs validation, `[-]` closed task

## Outcome and scope

Ship one maintained set of rules, gallery, previews and save codecs through two entry points: standalone launches directly into the gallery; Apparat offers a sixth 💉 tab titled STMPCK. Play works offline without enrollment or purchases. Existing standalone installations, updates, saves, and Web builds remain usable. Target Android and Linux GUI first; keep Linux headless free of game dependencies. No general plugin engine, telemetry, social ranking, profile sync, billing, or Research gameplay. Sound, haptics, and sensory preference persistence are separate unfinished STMPCK work, not prerequisites or release claims.

Private implementation and Pixel 4a testing are authorized; publication is not. Apparat [README](../../README.md#hud) specifies five tabs and no MVP gameplay. [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md) Phases 2/4 cover HUD navigation, Phase 5 Android, Phase 8 GUI/runtime separation, and Phase 17 release evidence. These are regression obligations, not an existing arcade milestone. Post-MVP Track C concerns research validation, not recreational games. Resolve this mismatch before tracked product implementation; do not silently change MVP completion criteria.

Estimate for one engineer: 2–4 engineering days to demonstrate the private Merge slice; roughly 3–6 weeks total for Android/Linux qualification, including broader integration and fixes. Not a deadline: revise after testing engine compatibility, physical input and release access.
